## About RBAF

The Royal British Air Force is a fictional Roblox group modeled on the traditions and discipline of the real RAF. We operate as an elite air division within Navy Sim on Roblox (SEA), focusing on structured ranks, coordinated gameplay, and a tight-knit community of dedicated players.

### Our Values
- **Honour** — We uphold the highest standards of conduct and professionalism
- **Discipline** — Structured ranks and coordinated operations
- **Aerial Supremacy** — Precision flying and tactical excellence
