Who We Are

Forged in
British Tradition

The Royal British Air Force (RBAF) is a fictional Roblox group and elite air division operating within Navy Sim on Roblox (SEA). Modelled on the traditions and discipline of the historic Royal Air Force, we bring order and aerial dominance to the seas.

A tight-knit community of dedicated players who value teamwork, structured ranks, and coordinated gameplay. Whether you're a seasoned pilot or a new recruit, the RBAF has a place for you.

Our Role in Navy Sim

What the RBAF Does

01
Air Superiority
The RBAF serves as the dedicated air arm of the SEA military — providing aerial cover, strike capability, and reconnaissance across naval engagements in Navy Sim on Roblox.
02
SEA Military
We operate as a division within the broader SEA military structure — working alongside other branches and taking orders through the chain of command.
03
Ranked Structure
From fresh recruits to senior officers, every RBAF member holds a rank and is expected to carry out their duties with discipline and pride.
04
Organised Events
Regular drills, patrols, and combat operations are held within Navy Sim. Members are expected to attend and participate actively.
05
British Heritage
Modelled on the real Royal Air Force, the RBAF brings authentic British military culture to Roblox naval gaming.
06
Discord Community
All coordination, announcements, and promotions happen through our Discord. Join us to stay in the loop and connect with the community.
